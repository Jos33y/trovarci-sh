## When to look past MaxBulk Mailer {#why-switch}

MaxBulk Mailer is a long-running program. People usually look elsewhere for one of these reasons:

- **You want to pay less.** MaxBulk is $99.95 per user. Trovarcis Sender is {{sender_price}} and covers 3 computers.
- **You want the program to stop you before a bad send.** A desktop sender that checks your list and your message first saves you from bounces and broken merge fields that hurt your sending account.
- **You want to send from Gmail without installing anything.** Then a Gmail add-on fits better than any desktop app.

## 2. Atomic Mail Sender {#atomic-mail-sender}

**Best for:** sending without an SMTP account of your own.

AtomPark's Windows sender can send through its built-in server or through your SMTP, with personalization, spin text and an unsubscribe wizard.

**Price:** $89.90 once, per PC. **Good to know:** mail sent from a built-in server leaves from your own internet connection, which mail providers often distrust, so most people still send through an SMTP account.

## 3. SendBlaster {#sendblaster}

**Best for:** people who already own a license.

A Windows sender with a free version and a Pro license. The free version sends to 100 recipients at a time and keeps 2 lists.

**Price:** $129 once for Pro. **Good to know:** the latest version, 4.4.2, is from April 2020.

## 4. Mailmeteor {#mailmeteor}

**Best for:** a few hundred emails a day from Gmail or Outlook.

A Gmail and Google Sheets add-on that sends through your own Gmail, Google Workspace or Outlook account.

**Price:** free for 50 emails a day, then $5.99 to $35.99 per user per month. **Good to know:** Gmail's limits still apply: 500 a day on a personal account, 2,000 on Google Workspace.

## 5. GMass {#gmass}

**Best for:** cold outreach with automatic follow-ups.

Works inside Gmail in Chrome, with mail merge from Google Sheets, plus follow-up sequences and A/B tests on its higher plans.

**Price:** from $29.95 a month, or $249 a year. **Good to know:** over two years, that is several times the price of a one-time desktop license.

## 6. Mailchimp {#mailchimp}

**Best for:** designed newsletters and sign-up forms.

A web platform that keeps your list and sends from its own servers, with templates and automations.

**Price:** free up to 250 contacts and 500 emails a month, then monthly by contact count. **Good to know:** the bill grows with every contact you keep, even ones you rarely email.

## How to choose {#how-to-choose}

- **Sending your own lists through your own accounts:** Trovarcis Sender.
- **Only from Gmail, small volumes:** Mailmeteor. **Outreach with follow-ups:** GMass.
- **Newsletters to subscribers:** Mailchimp.
