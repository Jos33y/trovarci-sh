## Why people look for an Atomic Mail Sender alternative {#why-switch}

- **One license per PC.** Using it on a desktop and a laptop means two licenses: AtomPark lists two PCs at $152.84.
- **The built-in server is risky for most senders.** Sending straight from your own internet connection skips the reputation of a real mail account, and mail from home connections is often blocked by mail providers. A tool built around your own SMTP account avoids that.
- **The trial is short.** It runs for 7 days and sends up to 50 emails a day.

## 1. Trovarcis Sender {#trovarcis-sender}

Trovarcis Sender is a Windows app you pay for once. It sends only through your own SMTP accounts, such as Gmail, Google Workspace, Zoho, Amazon SES, SendGrid, Brevo, Mailgun, Postmark or your own server, so every email carries the reputation of a real account.

**Price:** {{sender_price}} once, for 3 computers. Every 1.x update is included, with a 15-day refund if it doesn't work for you.

## 2. MaxBulk Mailer {#maxbulk-mailer}

**Best for:** Mac users who need a desktop sender today.

A mail merge and bulk email program for Mac and Windows that sends through your own mail server or an SMTP service, with attachments, test messages and a send-rate limit.

**Price:** $99.95 once, per user. **Good to know:** priced per user, so a team pays for each person.

## 3. SendBlaster {#sendblaster}

**Best for:** people who already own a license.

A Windows sender with a free version and a Pro license. The free version sends to 100 recipients at a time and keeps 2 lists.

**Price:** $129 once for Pro. **Good to know:** the latest version, 4.4.2, is from April 2020.

## 4. Mailmeteor {#mailmeteor}

**Best for:** small sends from Gmail or Outlook, no install.

Sends through your own Gmail, Google Workspace or Outlook account from a Gmail or Google Sheets add-on.

**Price:** free for 50 emails a day, then $5.99 to $35.99 per user per month. **Good to know:** Gmail caps you at 500 a day on a personal account and 2,000 on Google Workspace.

## 5. GMass {#gmass}

**Best for:** cold outreach with follow-ups.

A Gmail add-on for Chrome with mail merge from Google Sheets, plus follow-up sequences and A/B tests on its higher plans.

**Price:** from $29.95 a month, or $249 a year. **Good to know:** a monthly cost for as long as you send.

## 6. Mailchimp {#mailchimp}

**Best for:** newsletters to people who signed up.

A web platform that keeps your list and sends from its own servers.

**Price:** free up to 250 contacts and 500 emails a month, then monthly by contact count. **Good to know:** you pay for the contacts you keep, not the emails you send.

## How to choose {#how-to-choose}

- **Your own lists, more than one computer:** Trovarcis Sender, on Windows today, with a Mac version on the way.
- **Only from Gmail:** Mailmeteor for small sends, GMass for outreach.
- **Newsletters to subscribers:** Mailchimp.
