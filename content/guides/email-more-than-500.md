## 1. Know your limits

| Account | People per day | Per email |
|---|---|---|
| Personal Gmail | 500 | 500 |
| Google Workspace | 2,000 | Up to 500 outside your organization |
| Workspace free trial | 500 | 500 |

The day is a rolling 24 hours, not midnight to midnight, and your everyday emails count too.

## 2. Don't split it by hand

Pasting 500 addresses at a time into BCC works until it doesn't: one batch goes twice, one never goes, and a slip into CC shows every address. Over the limit, Gmail stops you sending anything, replies included, for up to 24 hours.

## 3. Send one email per person, paced

A sending app connected to your Gmail account sends each person their own email and stops at the daily number you set. A list of 1,800 from Workspace goes out in one day; from personal Gmail, over four days, or in one day through four accounts.

Set the daily number below the limit, say 400 for personal Gmail, so your normal email still goes through.
