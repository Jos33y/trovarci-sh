## 1. Export the list

From your spreadsheet or the tool you send from, export the addresses you're about to email as a CSV. Check the list you'll actually send to, not every contact you've ever had.

## 2. Upload it

Open the [Email Verifier](/verify), choose **Have a list?** and upload the file or paste the addresses. You can leave the page while it works; the list is saved on your dashboard.

## 3. Sort the results

| Result | What to do |
|---|---|
| Valid | Keep |
| Invalid | Remove. Each one would bounce |
| Risky | Keep shared inboxes you know. Drop throwaway inboxes. Send carefully to accept-all servers |
| Unknown | Check again later, or leave out of this send |

## 4. Import the clean list

Download **valid.txt** for only the valid addresses, or **results.csv** for every address with its result, and import the clean list into the tool you send from. Results are deleted after 48 hours, so download them now.
