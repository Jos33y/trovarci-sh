## 1. A separate domain for outreach

Never use the domain your invoices and customer emails come from. Register a close variation, like getnorthstar.com or northstarhq.com, for about $10 to $15 a year. If outreach ever hurts its reputation, your main email is untouched.

## 2. Real mailboxes, 2 to 5 per domain

Google Workspace Business Starter costs about $7 per mailbox a month on a yearly plan. Use real names, like anna@getnorthstar.com. Sending services such as SendGrid or Brevo ban cold email, so don't use them for this.

Set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC for the domain: the [DNS Generator](/records) writes the records, and the [Domain Checker](/domain) confirms them.

## 3. Warm up for 2 to 4 weeks

Use the new mailboxes like a person would: sign up to a few newsletters, email colleagues and friends, reply to what comes back. A brand-new mailbox sending 50 cold emails on its first day looks like what it is.

## 4. Small numbers, many mailboxes

| Mailboxes | Emails a day | Cost a month |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | About 120 | About $25 |
| 6 on 2 domains | About 250 | About $50 |

30 to 50 a day per mailbox. Reach more by adding mailboxes, not by sending faster.

## 5. Emails people don't report

- **Check the list first.** Bounces are the fastest way to lose a mailbox: run it through the [Email Verifier](/verify).
- **Plain text, one link at most, no attachments.**
- **One line written for that person.**
- **An easy way out**: "Not relevant? Reply 'no' and I won't write again." Then don't.

Workspace forbids spam too: personal, relevant, low-volume outreach is what keeps mailboxes alive.
