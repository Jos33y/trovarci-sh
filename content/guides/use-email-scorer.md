## 1. Paste the email

Open the [Email Scorer](/score). Choose **Simple** to paste the text of your email, or **HTML** to paste the code your email tool exports. Add the **Subject line** too: it's scored separately. Keep it under 60 characters.

## 2. Score it

Press **Score Email**. It takes a few seconds and costs 1 credit (1 cent). It checks the subject line, the writing, the layout, the links, and the unsubscribe link and postal address that bulk email needs.

## 3. Read the score

| Score | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 90 to 100 | Excellent | Nothing in the email itself should hurt you |
| 70 to 89 | Good | Small fixes left |
| 50 to 69 | Needs Work | Several things likely to hurt delivery |
| 30 to 49 | Poor | Likely to land in spam |
| Under 30 | Critical | Fix before sending |

## 4. Fix, then score again

Each problem shows a **Fix**. Start with the critical ones, change your email, then press **Re-Score Email** to see the new score next to the old one.

A high score covers the email only. If your domain isn't set up, a perfect email can still go to spam: run the [Domain Checker](/domain) too.
