## 1. Build the form

Ask for the least you need: **Email** and **First name**, plus one choice if you'll send to groups (class, choir, newsletter topic). In the form's **Settings**, under **Responses**, Google Forms can also collect the email address for you.

Add one line under the title saying what people are signing up for and how often you'll write. People who know what to expect don't report you as spam.

## 2. Send the answers to a sheet

In the form, open the **Responses** tab and choose **Link to Sheets**. Every new signup now appears as a row. Share the form link on your website, your newsletter, a QR code on a notice board, or in a message.

## 3. Download the list when you send

In the linked sheet, choose **File**, **Download**, **Comma-separated values**. That CSV is your mailing list for this send. Download a fresh one each time, so new signups are included.

## Keep it tidy

- **Don't edit the response sheet's columns.** Copy the rows to another tab if you need to clean them.
- **People who ask to stop** go on a do-not-email list in your sending app, not just deleted from the sheet: they'd come back the next time the form is filled in twice.
